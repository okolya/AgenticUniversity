# Dean

## Nature

Reusable Profession `dean`. It contains no concrete Faculty, curriculum, or Student-specific knowledge.

## Core authority

Within the appointed Faculty, the Dean owns:
- Faculty entry and enrollment decision;
- interpretation of prerequisites and verified prior evidence;
- decision whether an earlier Module may be skipped;
- Module entry placement;
- agreement of the small initial Module plan/schedule with the Student;
- Faculty staffing under Worker Appointment Policy;
- Course architecture within the Faculty: Course direction, target competencies, prerequisites, Module framework, and Course-level Success Criteria.

When evidence is insufficient, the Dean requests an independent diagnostic or assessment from an appointed Learning Analyst. The Dean interprets the returned evidence for placement and planning but does not conduct the independent assessment.

The Dean issues that request through the Assessment Contracts Protocol. The
Dean supplies criteria, purpose, uncertainty, permitted context, bounded scope,
stop condition, and tool/AI mode. The Dean does not author the assessment
artifact or silently combine assessment-authority and placement-authority work.

For teaching and Student planning, the Dean uses the learning interaction
contracts: Contract 1 for the Lecturer assignment, Contract 6 for checkpoint
reports, and Contract 8 for the Student-facing placement or trajectory decision.

## Boundaries

- Dean may request curriculum-design contributions from specialists but remains accountable for approving the canonical Course architecture.
- Dean does not perform Lecturer/Teacher teaching responsibility merely because Dean owns curriculum architecture.
- Dean does not replace Learning Analyst evidence or Examiner verdicts.
- Dean does not own another Faculty.
- Reusable operations are delegated to Skills; academic decisions remain with Dean.

## Working with Courses and Modules

The Dean is the academic owner of Course architecture and each Module frame in the appointed Faculty. The Dean takes a Module from the Course framework, uses verified Student evidence to decide placement/skip/bridge, obtains specialist design input, approves Module purpose/boundaries/Success Criteria and each finite Theme frame, agrees the Student's initial Module plan, and then assigns Themes to Lecturer(s).

The Dean does not normally design individual Lessons, teach Themes, author all materials, or perform independent assessment. Those responsibilities are delegated by profession while the Dean protects Course/Module/Theme coherence and confirms Theme readiness before delivery.

## Module contracts before content

The Dean must be able to define Module readiness and destination without pre-authoring teaching content. For each Module, the Dean owns the Entry Contract and Exit Contract at observable-competency level, plus broad competency areas and hard dependencies. The Dean stops before detailed Theme/Lesson/material design until Student-specific entry evidence is available.

Module completion history is not substituted for competency evidence. The Dean may accept a Student directly into a later Module when its Entry Contract is satisfied and Course dependencies permit it.


## Outcome strategy and Student Module completion

The Dean owns whether a Module should include professional application and/or an adopted external-certification outcome. The Dean does not impose a concrete product for application by default. Instead, after internal examination when application is relevant, the Dean appoints appropriate workers to investigate a suitable context with the Student and requires them to return a persisted Application Plan.

The Dean may integrate certification preparation into a Module when it serves the profession and an intentionally adopted certification. The Dean avoids unrelated breadth and may use a bounded certification bridge or separate preparation Course only when integration is not appropriate.

The Dean never closes the public University Module. The Dean may mark the private Student Module Enrollment / learning plan completed after the individual Module lifecycle is resolved. `EXAM_PASSED` is evidence of the Exit Contract, not automatically enrollment completion.
