# Multimedia Curation — Thema 4 / Кураторський звіт мультимедіа — Тема 4

- **Active Profession:** Lecturer, Language Faculty
- **Scope:** Module 1 → Theme 4 → Lesson 2, integrated four-skills cycle
- **Outcome supported:** extract a small set of location/time/action information from short everyday German and mark the listening practice as `attested`
- **Learner language/level:** German learner path with Ukrainian support; A2 bridge
- **Use mode:** optional supplementary listening only; the original Lesson message remains the primary material and the evidence burden does not increase
- **Student context:** no Student-specific context supplied; duplication and fit must be confirmed at delivery

## Proposal M4-1

- **Status:** `optional`
- **Type:** audio
- **Source role:** supplementary
- **Source:** [Goethe-Zertifikat A2 — Hören, Modellsatz](https://bfu.goethe.de/a2_mod_2MX5/hoeren.php?pane2=zu), Goethe-Institut
- **Exact fragment:** Teil 1, Aufgabe 3, “Wo findet das Treffen statt?”; direct audio link: `https://bfu.goethe.de/medien/a2_2005/audio_a2_1_3.mp4` (the source page lists the clip as 0.2 MB and states that Part 1 texts are heard twice)
- **Learner action:** listen up to two times and identify the meeting location from the three provided choices; do not count the result as independent assessment
- **Expected observable result:** the learner marks the relevant place and records completion as `attested`; the Lecturer may then ask for one short spoken or written response using the Lesson’s functional phrases
- **Accessibility:** source page offers audio plus sign-language and lip-reading video alternatives for the A2 listening module; no transcript was verified for this individual clip
- **Access/licensing:** link-only proposal; public Goethe page and media URL were reachable during curation, but reuse/embedding permission was not verified. Do not download, rehost, or copy the audio.
- **Risks:** external URL or media format may fail; the clip is assessment-like and its exact vocabulary may not match the Student’s Entry gap; it must not become a certification task.
- **Fallback:** the original Lesson 2 message read aloud by a partner or by the Lecturer, exactly twice, with the existing `attested` note.

## Decision

Keep M4-1 optional and link-only. Do not insert it as a required resource until the Lecturer confirms learner fit at delivery and the Instructional Assistant reviews the changed learner path. The original self-contained Lesson remains sufficient without external media.
