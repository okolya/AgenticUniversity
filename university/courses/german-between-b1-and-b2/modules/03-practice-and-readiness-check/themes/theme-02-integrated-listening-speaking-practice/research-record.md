# Research Record — Thema 2 / Дослідницький запис — Тема 2

- **Aktiver Worker:** Ferdinand, Lecturer, Language Faculty
- **Scope:** Modul 3 / Thema 2 — Integrierte Hören/Sprechen-Praxis
- **Purpose:** decide whether a public audio fragment supports prescribed listening practice without implying measurement
- **Dialogue-/Lernsprache:** Deutsch, Zielniveau B1
- **Policy/mode:** ahead-of-student; evidence class `attested` only

## Sources checked

1. [Goethe-Institut: Goethe-Zertifikat B1](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/prf/gzb1.html) — level boundary retained; exam listening formats rejected as Lesson content.
2. [Deutsche Welle: Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten](https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/nachrichten/s-8030) — optional supplementary listening channel; unstable daily fragment; not copied into Lessons.
3. Generic B1 Prüfungstraining Hörverstehen packs — rejected: exam simulation and measurement framing conflict with `attested`-only Theme boundary.

## Decision

Lessons use original short dialogue scripts. Optional DW/Goethe audio may be offered only as a substitute input channel when the learner can still complete the same attestation checklist; the default fallback is a partner or the Student reading the script aloud once at natural speed. No external audio is required to complete the Theme. No multimedia is mandatory.

## Verification boundary

No external audio is hosted or transcribed into University material beyond the original scripts authored here. Attestation records completion of practice, never listening quality or CEFR score.
