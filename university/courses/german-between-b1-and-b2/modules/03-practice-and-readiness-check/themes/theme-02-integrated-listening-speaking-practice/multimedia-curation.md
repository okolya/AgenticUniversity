## Proposal (optional)

- **Source class:** public news-for-learners channel (e.g. DW Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten)
- **Intended use:** one short familiar-topic clip as an alternative Hörinput for Lesson 1 or 3 when the learner prefers authentic audio
- **Learner action:** listen once or twice, then complete the same attestation checklist as for the script fallback
- **Accessibility / risks:** daily clips change; may be too fast or topic-mismatched; may be unavailable offline
- **Fallback (required):** partner or Student reads the Lesson script aloud once at natural speed; attestation still records prescribed practice completion

## Bound

Optional audio never upgrades evidence to `demonstrated`, never shortens practice, and never replaces the attestation checklist. Laboratory Specialist is not required: practice stays Lesson-embedded.
