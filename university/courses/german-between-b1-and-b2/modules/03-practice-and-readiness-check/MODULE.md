# Module 3 — Practice and Readiness Check

## Identity

- **Course:** Між B1 та B2
- **Faculty:** Language Faculty
- **Module position:** 3 of 4
- **Status:** public competency frame; measured independently by the Learning
  Analyst role (current staffing: see `COURSE.md`)

## Purpose and value

Turn recovered B1 knowledge into repeatable performance across all four
skills. The Module is not a second theory course: it exposes transfer gaps,
corrects them in bounded cycles, and produces evidence for the B2-readiness
decision.

## Entry Contract

The Student can independently demonstrate, at least in familiar and controlled
contexts, the competencies in Exit criteria 1–7 of Module 2 (B1 Recovery), with
any known limitations explicitly recorded. The evidence is current enough to
design practice and measurement against the Course Success Criteria. Completing
Module 2 is not required.

## Exit Contract

The Student can independently:

1. complete bounded B1-level tasks in Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, and Sprechen;
2. maintain understandable communication across familiar situations without
   relying on isolated sentence patterns;
3. produce connected spoken and written responses with appropriate structure;
4. use feedback to revise a response and demonstrate improvement on the same
   criterion;
5. identify, against the B1 criteria, the limitations that remain in their own
   performance;
6. produce the evidence for criteria 1–5 without assistance beyond the stated
   tool and AI-use mode.

## Broad competency areas

- integrated four-skill performance;
- transfer from controlled exercises to unfamiliar but bounded contexts;
- fluency, comprehensibility, accuracy, and task completion;
- revision, feedback use, and error-pattern control;
- independent evidence collection and interpretation.

## Scope exclusions

This Module does not teach B2 outcomes and does not issue a B2 verdict. It
measures readiness against the approved B1 boundary and returns unresolved
gaps to bounded correction or to the Dean.

## Practice and checkpoint

A final checkpoint consolidates the evidence but does not replace practice
embedded in the preceding Modules. Independent measurement is performed by the
Learning Analyst role and the Module verdict is issued by the Examiner role.

## Source provenance

The official [Goethe B1 descriptors and practice materials](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/prf/gzb1.html)
provide the reference boundary for four-skill B1 performance. They are not
adopted as an exam-specific format for this Course.
