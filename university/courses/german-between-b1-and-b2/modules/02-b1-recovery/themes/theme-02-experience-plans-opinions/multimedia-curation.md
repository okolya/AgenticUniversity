# Multimedia-Curation — Thema 2

- **Aktiver Worker:** Ferdinand, Lecturer, Language Faculty
- **Scope:** Modul 2 — B1-Wiederherstellung / Thema 2 — Erfahrungen, Pläne, Meinungen und Erklärungen
- **Dialogue-/Lernsprache:** German; Ukrainian orientation in the source files
- **Status:** explicit `no-multimedia` decision for ahead-of-student preparation

## Decision

No external audio, image, or video is required for the four approved Lesson slots. The Theme's evidence delta is controlled connected production: the learner must select and connect their own experience, plan, reasons, consequence, opinion, counterpoint, and revision. An external clip would add listening or interpretation work that is not needed to expose these criteria and could distract from the bounded production sequence.

## Fallback and accessibility

The Lessons are self-contained Markdown and offer either writing or a learner's own voice recording where appropriate. The Lecturer may read the original prompts aloud or provide a plain-text copy; no platform, login, caption track, region access, or live media is required. If a future Student needs listening support, the Lecturer may propose one bounded clip through a new curation decision without adding a Lesson slot.

## Rejected media path

Goethe-Institut B1 speaking and discussion pages were considered as optional reference material, but no exact audio/video fragment was necessary to meet the approved outcome. The relevant phrase and level sources are recorded in `research-record.md`; they are not multimedia dependencies.

## Boundary

This is a curation decision, not a claim that no multimedia could ever help a particular Student. A Student-specific accessibility need or a concrete listening gap would trigger a new bounded curation handoff and Lecturer decision.
