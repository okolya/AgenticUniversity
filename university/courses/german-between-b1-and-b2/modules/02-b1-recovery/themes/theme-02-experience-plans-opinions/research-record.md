# Research Record — Thema 2 / Дослідницький запис — Тема 2

- **Aktiver Worker:** Ferdinand, Lecturer, Language Faculty
- **Scope:** Modul 2 — B1-Wiederherstellung / Thema 2 — Erfahrungen, Pläne, Meinungen und Erklärungen
- **Purpose:** source check for connected B1 production, functional connectors, reasons, consequences and simple opinion; no new Lesson slot
- **Dialogue-/Lernsprache:** German learner path with Ukrainian orientation; target level B1
- **Policy/mode:** ahead-of-student preparation; sources are references and are not copied into the Lessons

## Sources checked

1. [Goethe-Institut: Deutsch B1](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/uun/dln/db1.html) — retained as a level/outcome reference. The page states that B1 learners can describe experiences and events in connected sentences and briefly explain and justify opinions and plans. The Lessons translate that broad descriptor into the approved Theme's bounded criteria; the page is not reproduced as a learner task.
2. [Goethe-Institut: Redemittel Meinung ausdrücken, zustimmen, widersprechen](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ua/prj/eur/mmo/priv/14952524-STANDARD.pdf) — retained as a reference for *Ich denke/finde/glaube*, *Meiner Meinung nach*, *weil*, agreement and disagreement language. Only short functional expressions needed by Lesson 3 are adapted; the PDF is not copied.
3. [Goethe-Institut: Deutsch Online B1 — Übersicht Redemittel und Grammatik](https://lernplattform.goethe.de/pluginfile.php/1499547/mod_folder/content/0/DTonlineB1_GR-RM_Rueckschau_DE.pdf) — checked for experience-reporting and positive/negative opinion language. Used as a cross-check for natural B1 phrasing; not required for delivery.
4. [Goethe-Zertifikat B1 Modellsatz — Sprechen](https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/sprechen.php) — rejected as a primary teaching source. It is examination-format material, while Module 2 explicitly excludes exam-specific strategies. Its broad topic structure confirms relevance only; no exam task is assigned.

## Decision

The retained sources support level calibration and phrase selection. The four Lessons remain original and self-contained because the approved Theme needs a gradual connected-production sequence rather than copied exam tasks or a dependency on external access.

## Verification boundary

Public page/PDF links and their visible descriptions were checked. This record does not certify every external pedagogical or factual claim, copyright permission beyond the precise references, or the Student's future performance. No external passage is reproduced.
