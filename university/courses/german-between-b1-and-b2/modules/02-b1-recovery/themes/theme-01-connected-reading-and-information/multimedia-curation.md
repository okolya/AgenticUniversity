# Multimedia-Curation — Thema 1

- **Aktiver Worker:** Ferdinand, Lecturer, Language Faculty
- **Scope:** Modul 2 — B1-Wiederherstellung / Thema 1 — Zusammenhängendes Lesen und Informationen entnehmen
- **Dialogue-/Lernsprache:** Deutsch; ukrainische Orientierung im Material
- **Ziel:** ergänzende authentische Lese- und Wortschatzexposition auf B1, ohne die vier genehmigten Lesson-Slots zu erweitern

## Proposal

- **status:** `optional`
- **type:** `image` plus linked reading material
- **URL / source:** [Goethe-Institut Deutschstunde — „War das schön …“](https://www.goethe.de/prj/dlp/de/unterrichtsmaterial/reihe/urlaub_und_reisen/war_das_schoen)
- **creator:** Goethe-Institut, Deutschstunde Portal
- **source role:** `supplementary`
- **language / level:** Deutsch / B1; Jugendliche und Erwachsene
- **exact fragment:** page section “Lesetext ‘War das schön’”, linked PDF `lesetext_b1_war-das-schoen.pdf`; use only the first short reading passage selected by the Lecturer after opening the PDF, not the full packet
- **supported outcome:** Theme criterion 1–3: identify main idea, extract details, infer topic vocabulary
- **learner action:** read the selected short passage, write one main-idea sentence, record three relevant details, and infer one word from context; do not complete the source’s exam-format tasks
- **expected observable result:** a four-line reading note that can be compared with the same Theme criteria
- **accessibility:** webpage identifies the material as B1 and provides a downloadable PDF; text is available visually, but screen-reader quality and exact page location must be checked before delivery
- **access / attribution:** public Goethe-Institut page; keep the link and attribution, do not copy the PDF into University material, and do not claim permission beyond the public download
- **risks:** PDF may be too long or include exam-oriented tasks outside this Theme; page number and screen-reader behavior are unresolved until direct PDF inspection
- **fallback:** use the original short texts in Lessons 1–4 and read them aloud or provide plain Markdown text; no external platform or login is required

## Decision boundary

This is a proposal, not approved learner-facing material. The Lecturer may use the link only after checking the PDF fragment and accessibility. The core Lessons remain self-contained without it.
