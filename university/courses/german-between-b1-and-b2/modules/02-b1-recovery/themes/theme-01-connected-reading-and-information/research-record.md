## Decision

The Goethe-Institut “War das schön …” page is retained as an `optional supplementary` proposal in `multimedia-curation.md`. The four Lessons remain self-contained with original texts, so a broken link, PDF accessibility problem, or unsuitable fragment does not block the Theme.

## Verification boundary

The page-level B1 metadata and download links were checked. Exact PDF page number, screen-reader behavior, and reuse terms for a selected fragment remain unresolved until the Lecturer opens the PDF immediately before optional delivery. No external passage is reproduced in University material.
