## Teaching-intent plan

Die Sequenz ist **Nachfragen → Missverständnis reparieren → Transfer und
Selbstkorrektur → Module checkpoint**. Lesson 1 introduces a repair move that
keeps interaction moving. Lesson 2 adds reformulation and correction after a
meaning break. Lesson 3 requires independent selection of the appropriate move
in a changed familiar context and one visible self-correction. Lesson 4
integrates the sequence and returns a bounded Module handoff.
