## Instructional Assistant review

The complete package was inspected as one bounded Theme package. The review
inspected the four Lessons together, not only individual wording, and checked:

- German learner path versus Ukrainian orientation;
- first-use explanations of `Register`, `Betreff`, `Anrede`, `Schlussformel`
  and `Überarbeitung`;
- sequence integrity: structure → register → revision → checkpoint;
- stable writing schema: Adressat, Zweck, Schlüsselinformation, Abschluss;
- whether uncertainty (`?`) and actual correction (`[geändert]`) are clearly
  distinct;
- whether each task requests only the evidence needed for its declared delta;
- whether Lesson 4 is genuinely a new familiar situation rather than a renamed
  repetition;
- whether the package reaches the Theme outcome without a fifth slot.
