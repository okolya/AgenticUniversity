## Teaching-intent plan

Die Sequenz ist absichtlich vierstufig: **Struktur → Register → begrenzte
Überarbeitung → Checkpoint**. Lesson 1 establishes the stable writing frame
(Adressat, Zweck, Schlüsselinformation, Abschluss). Lesson 2 adds recipient and
register choice without changing the communicative purpose. Lesson 3 separates
uncertainty, observation and correction and limits revision to one visible
form. Lesson 4 transfers the complete sequence to a new familiar situation and
returns the bounded evidence to the Dean.

Multimedia is not anticipated: the outcome is precise short writing and visible
revision, and a self-contained text package gives a lower evidence burden and a
usable fallback.

## Research → multimedia → authoring

- **Research decision:** no external learner material retained. The Theme needs
  short, controlled examples with exact wording and a stable evidence format;
  original examples are therefore more traceable than a generic external
  worksheet. No external text has been copied or adapted.
- **Multimedia decision:** `no-multimedia`. Audio/video/image is not needed to
  demonstrate the declared writing and revision deltas. Fallback is the
  self-contained Markdown package and the same task in plain text.
- **Authoring:** all four Lessons, examples, prompts, tables and Ukrainian
  orientation notes were authored for these bounded slots.
- **Language distinction:** German is the target learner path. Ukrainian is an
  orientation layer that explains task logic and terms; it is not a second
  competing German exercise path. Delivery language remains session-specific.
