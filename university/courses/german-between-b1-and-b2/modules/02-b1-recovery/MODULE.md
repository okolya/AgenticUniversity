# Module 2 — B1 Recovery

## Identity

- **Course:** Між B1 та B2
- **Faculty:** Language Faculty
- **Module position:** 2 of 4
- **Status:** public competency frame; detailed Themes open only at Module Entry

## Purpose and value

Rebuild and stabilize the B1 competencies required for independent
communication after a long interruption of study. The Module connects grammar
and vocabulary to reading, listening, speaking, and writing instead of
treating them as isolated recall targets.

## Entry Contract

The Student can, in familiar contexts and according to current verified
evidence:

1. form understandable basic statements and questions with controlled word
   order;
2. preserve meaning with essential articles, cases, pronouns, prepositions, and
   common verb forms;
3. refer to present, past, and future situations in a comprehensible way;
4. understand and use high-frequency vocabulary and functional phrases.

The evidence may come from A2 Recovery or from any other current verified
source. Completing Module 1 is not required.

## Exit Contract

The Student can independently:

1. understand the main points and relevant details of clear standard German
   on familiar everyday, study, and work-related topics;
2. read connected B1-level texts and identify their main message, supporting
   information, and relevant vocabulary;
3. speak coherently about experiences, plans, opinions, and familiar problems,
   with enough interactional control to maintain a conversation;
4. write connected practical texts with an understandable structure and
   appropriate B1-level language;
5. use the B1 grammar and vocabulary needed for meaning, while identifying
   and correcting recurring personal errors;
6. transfer a recovered form or expression from controlled practice into a
   new but familiar communicative context;
7. notice uncertainty in a spoken or written attempt and request clarification
   or repair when communication breaks down.

## Broad competency areas

- comprehension of connected spoken and written language;
- narration, description, explanation, and simple opinion;
- practical correspondence and connected writing;
- B1 grammar in communicative use;
- vocabulary expansion, retrieval, and register awareness;
- interaction, clarification, repair, and self-correction.

## Scope exclusions

This Module does not teach B2 complexity, academic or professional
specialization, or exam-specific strategies. It does not repeat demonstrated
competencies solely to fill a fixed Lesson count.

## Practice and checkpoint

Each Theme or later Lesson must add a new capability, independence level, or
transfer context. The checkpoint returns demonstrated criteria, unresolved gaps,
and the evidence delta required by the next Module.
