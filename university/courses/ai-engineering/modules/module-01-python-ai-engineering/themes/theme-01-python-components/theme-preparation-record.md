# Theme 1 — Preparation and handoff record

## Scope

This record covers preparation of Theme 1 — Python components and maintainable
boundaries. The approved sequence contains exactly four Lessons and one final
Theme checkpoint.

## Completed workflow

1. **Dean Profession:** approved Theme outcome, criteria, exclusions, four-Lesson
   map, checkpoint, and stop condition.
2. **Lecturer Profession:** prepared the four Lessons, practical evidence
   contracts, and teaching sequence inside the approved frame.
3. **Teacher Profession:** prepared a bounded reinforcement plan. It is dormant
   until a concrete learning gap appears.
4. **Laboratory Specialist Profession:** prepared one optional Lab for Lesson 4;
   it does not add a Lesson or change Theme scope.
5. **Learning Analyst Profession:** mapped observable evidence and the independence
   boundary. No independent assessment was requested or conducted.
6. **Examiner Profession:** mapped Theme evidence to later Module examinability.
   No examination or verdict was started.
7. **Instructional Assistant Profession / resolved University-wide appointment:** reviewed the
   complete Theme materials. Findings were applied by the Lecturer.
8. **Lecturer Profession:** completed final control review of the full Theme,
   including outcome, vocabulary, tasks, evidence burden, AI-use mode, and
   handoff boundaries.

## Delivery status

Theme 1 is ready for Student delivery. The next handoff is Student response to
the assigned Lecturer under Learning Interaction Contract 3. The Lecturer owns
Lesson feedback. Teacher or Laboratory Specialist may be invoked only for a
concrete gap or practical need. Learning Analyst and Examiner
remain outside formative Lesson verdicts.

## Stop and next decision

After Lesson 4, the Lecturer sends Contract 6 evidence to the Dean. The Dean
decides whether
the Theme criteria are demonstrated and whether to open the next Dean-approved
Theme, request a bounded correction, or request independent measurement. No
Worker may create an additional Theme 1 Lesson unilaterally.
