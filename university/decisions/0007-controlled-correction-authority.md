# ADR 0007: Controlled correction authority

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01

## Decision

Student criticism enters the Instructional Assistant coordination path. The
Lecturer owns subject correctness, scope, and impact assessment. The Dean owns
mandatory escalation resolution and may narrow the proposal with binding
execution instructions. The Assistant may execute only after the applicable
decision chain and only through bounded technical Skills.

The absolute 15% rule covers text growth, text reduction, and replaced
knowledge. The larger text or learning-impact assessment governs. Uncertainty,
zero baselines, and exactly 15% escalate to the Dean.

## Consequences

The Assistant cannot self-approve, change the threshold, broaden scope, or
edit non-material core files. A technical blocker is recorded without creating
an approval ping-pong loop.
