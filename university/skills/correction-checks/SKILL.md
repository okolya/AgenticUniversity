# Correction checks

Run the required core, relative-link, manifest, and changed-executable checks
after applying an approved correction and again after conflict resolution or
further edits.

A failed or unavailable check blocks merge. The result must identify the
source revision, changed material targets, checks run, and failure reason
without copying private dialogue or Student/Worker identities.
