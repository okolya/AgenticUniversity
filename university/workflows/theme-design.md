# Theme Design Workflow

Owner: appointed Lecturer assigned to the Theme.

Purpose: convert an approved Module Theme frame into a coherent teachable unit.

1. Dean sends the approved Theme frame to the assigned Lecturer through
   Learning Interaction Contract 1. The frame must contain the outcome,
   Success Criteria, exclusions, finite Lesson map, final checkpoint, and stop
   condition. Lecturer then loads the Course direction, Module purpose,
   permitted Student evidence, and current Module plan.
2. Before detailed development, Lecturer reports a short teaching-intent plan
   to the Dean: intended learner outcome, Theme boundary, ordered Lesson
   steps, expected progress evidence, and anticipated specialist contributions.
   The report cannot add slots or change criteria.
3. After the report, Lecturer asks the Student whether relevant materials are available for review, and receives any links, notes, code, books, articles, or other safely shareable learning artifacts before assembling the Theme and Lesson.
4. Lecturer evaluates Student-provided materials for relevance, quality, currency, provenance, accessibility, and suitability; decides whether to include, adapt, mark as optional context, or exclude each item. Student contribution does not automatically approve content or change the Module contract.
5. Lecturer verifies that the Dean-approved Theme outcome and Success Criteria
   are traceable to the Module criteria; the Lecturer may propose a change but
   does not redefine the Theme frame.
6. Lecturer identifies the concepts/skills that must be introduced and what
   may be omitted because it is already verified.
7. Lecturer prepares the Lessons named in the finite Lesson map. Lessons are
   learning steps, not arbitrary chunks of text; no new slot is created here.
8. Lecturer identifies where Teacher reinforcement and Laboratory Specialist practice are useful and requests those contributions without transferring Theme ownership.
9. Lecturer researches/curates existing high-quality materials before authoring replacements. New material is authored where external material is unsuitable, incomplete, inaccessible, outdated, or a custom explanation is educationally better.
10. Every Lesson records exact provenance for external or Student-provided material: URL or repository/file path, source role, specific sections/topics used, and whether the Lesson is original, adapted, or a reference only. A generic source name or unlinked reference is insufficient when a precise link/path is available.
11. Lecturer does not copy access-controlled or copyrighted material beyond applicable rights; the Lesson uses original explanation, permitted excerpts, or a precise reference instead.
12. Lecturer prepares only the near-term Lessons/materials needed to start the Theme; later Lesson detail remains adaptive.
13. Before delivery, Lecturer invokes the Instructional Assistant Profession
    for a complete review of the full Theme package, including Lessons,
    practical tasks, evidence burden, and specialist-facing instructions.
14. Lecturer applies or rejects findings with a recorded disposition and
    performs a complete final control review. Dean then marks the Theme ready
    or returns it for a bounded correction.
15. If the Theme requires changing Module scope or outcomes, Lecturer proposes
    the change to Dean rather than modifying the Module silently.

## Specialist and Student interaction gates

- Teacher is requested only for a concrete anticipated reinforcement need and
  receives a bounded gap, not a new Theme.
- Laboratory Specialist is requested only for a practical Lab or experiment
  that is not already covered by the Lesson task.
- Learning Analyst may contribute measurability guidance, but works with the
  Student only after a complete Dean Assessment Request.
- Examiner may contribute examinability guidance, but works with the Student
  only during an approved Module examination.
- Instructional Assistant reviews material with its owner and does not teach or
  assess the Student.
- The normal Student path is Lecturer → Student. Every additional handoff
  names its trigger, scope, input, output, and stop condition.
- If a required Profession has no active Worker appointment, the workflow
  stops at the staffing boundary; no Worker is invented.
