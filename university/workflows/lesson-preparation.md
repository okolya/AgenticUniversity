# Lesson Preparation Workflow

Owner: appointed Lecturer for a lesson introducing new material.

1. Load the Theme outcome, relevant Success Criteria, current Student evidence, prior Lesson result, and AI-use policy.
2. Define one clear Lesson outcome: what the Student should understand or be able to do after this learning step.
3. Select the smallest coherent content needed for that outcome.
4. Curate external material first where it is high quality and suitable; record source and exact relevant section. Use `research-materials` when needed.
5. Use `author-material` for original explanations, examples, notes, diagrams/specifications, or bridging material that is actually needed.
6. Prepare formative questions/exercises sufficient to expose misunderstanding. These are teaching checks, not independent verified assessment.
7. If practice is substantial, request a Lab from Laboratory Specialist. If reinforcement/remediation is needed, hand the relevant outcome/gap to Teacher.
8. Deliver/record reusable public learning material where appropriate. Student mastery conclusions remain private Student state.
9. Do not expand Theme or Module scope from inside a Lesson.
