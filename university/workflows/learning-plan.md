# Learning Plan Workflow

There are two planning levels. Do not collapse them.

All Dean, Worker, and Student handoffs in this workflow use
`university/protocols/learning-interaction-contracts.md`. Assessment handoffs
also use `assessment-contracts.md`.

## Course learning route

Owner: Dean.

The public Course defines the stable route: direction, competencies, Module framework, dependencies, and Course Success Criteria. It is not a Student calendar and does not contain a fully generated Lesson plan.

## Student Module plan

Owner: Dean with Student.

Near Module entry, Dean uses verified evidence to define and agree:
- Module coverage required for this Student;
- skipped/already-verified areas;
- bridge needs;
- priority and bounded pre-entry Theme/Lesson sequence;
- evidence checkpoints, permitted Lesson slots, and stop/decision condition;
- a short realistic planning horizon.

The private Student workspace stores capacity, dates, actual progress, next actions, and adaptations.

## Theme/Lesson execution plan

Owner: Dean defines the bounded Theme sequence; Lecturer executes it inside the
approved Module plan until the Theme checkpoint and Dean handoff.

Lecturer prepares the near-term Lessons needed to execute the Dean-approved
sequence. Lecturer may adapt explanation, examples, Lesson depth, and local
order inside the assigned slots, but may not add slots, extend the sequence, or
change its stop condition. Any extension or new bridge need returns to Dean.
When the Theme sequence ends, Lecturer returns accumulated evidence; Dean
confirms Theme completion, opens the next approved Theme, requests bounded
correction, or routes the evidence to the authorized measurement/examination
workflow.

Teacher and Laboratory Specialist plan their own assigned reinforcement/lab sessions inside that frame.

## Adaptation

- New verified evidence may trigger Dean to change Module coverage/trajectory.
- Formative learning observations may trigger Lecturer/Teacher to adapt Lesson depth or sequence.
- Reaching a sequence checkpoint or exhausting its allowed slots triggers a
  Lecturer evidence handoff to Dean, not automatic creation of another Lesson
  or automatic Module Entry.
- A change to Course or Module outcomes goes back to Dean.
