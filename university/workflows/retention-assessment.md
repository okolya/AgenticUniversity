# Retention assessment

Owner: appointed Learning Analyst for the assessment scope.

1. Receive previously verified competencies and the retention criteria/time context.
2. Assess whether the Student can still demonstrate the required knowledge/skill without turning the session into teaching.
3. Record current evidence and any decay/uncertainty.
4. Return findings to the responsible planning/Faculty workflow.
5. Do not prescribe remediation; Teacher/Dean decide the next educational action within their authority.
