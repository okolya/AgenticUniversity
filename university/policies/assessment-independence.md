# Assessment independence

Independent measurement is an academic boundary, not merely a different Skill mode.

1. Lecturer, Teacher, and Laboratory Specialist may perform formative checks and produce learning observations, but these are not independent verified assessment evidence.
2. Learning Analyst owns independent diagnostics, prerequisite/competency verification, Theme assessment evidence, re-assessment, gap analysis, and retention measurement within the assigned scope.
3. Examiner owns the final internal Module-level academic verdict against Module Success Criteria.
4. A Worker who materially taught or coached the assessed scope must not act as Learning Analyst or Examiner for that same scope unless an explicit University exception is recorded before assessment.
5. Dean may request assessment and appoint assessment staff, but may not alter Learning Analyst evidence or Examiner verdict to force progression.
6. Rector may resolve institutional disputes or replace an assessor, but does not rewrite independent evidence/verdict as if Rector performed the assessment.
